# CyberTwin Generalization Audit (Milestone 3.5)

## 1. Motivation
The Random Forest classifier achieved a staggering F1-score of `0.9998` in Milestone 3. While high performance is desired, near-perfect scores on cybersecurity benchmark datasets frequently signal data leakage, trivial feature memorization, or non-generalizable artifacts rather than actual robust learning. This audit investigates the origins of this performance and assesses the true generalization capacity of the model.

## 2. Original Benchmark
- **Model**: Random Forest
- **Validation Split**: Random Stratified 15% Holdout
- **Results**:
  - Accuracy: 0.9998
  - F1-Score: 0.9998
  - ROC-AUC: 0.9999
  - PR-AUC: 0.9999

## 3. Duplicate Analysis
Exact raw-row duplicates were removed during preprocessing. However, we checked for feature-level duplicates across the Train and Test splits.
- **Findings**: There are only 36 exact feature-duplicates between Train and Test sets, representing 0.11% of the test set. Validation/Test duplicates account for 0.10%, and Train/Validation accounts for 0.06%. This definitively proves that the 0.9998 F1-score is **not** caused by exact raw data leakage.

## 4. Feature Dependence
- **Findings**: The Random Forest does not rely on a single dominant leaked feature. The importance is distributed smoothly across core packet size metrics.
- **Top 5 Features**:
  1. `Fwd Packet Length Max` (9.78%)
  2. `Subflow Fwd Bytes` (9.68%)
  3. `Total Length of Fwd Packets` (8.89%)
  4. `Avg Fwd Segment Size` (8.82%)
  5. `Packet Length Mean` (8.82%)

## 5. Feature Ablation
We subjected the Random Forest to severe feature ablation tests to see if the model relies entirely on single artifacts (like our engineered features) or if the separation is innate to the core protocol features.
- **Results**:
  - **Original 70 features**: F1 = 0.99985
  - **Without engineered features**: F1 = 0.99985
  - **Without top 3 features**: F1 = 0.99985
  - **Core behavioral-only (32 feats)**: F1 = 0.99988
- **Conclusion**: The dataset contains massive multi-collinear redundancy for PortScan signatures. Even after dropping the most critical features or retaining only 32 behavioral features, the model perfectly identifies the scanning profile.

## 6. Temporal Structure
**Limitation Encountered**: A true chronological holdout could not be constructed from the available processed dataset because a sufficiently reliable temporal grouping variable was unavailable in the raw `Friday-WorkingHours-Afternoon-PortScan.pcap_ISCX.csv` file (it utilizes a 79-column format that strictly omits `Timestamp` and `Flow ID`).

## 7. Group Structure
**Limitation Encountered**: Similarly, a group-aware split (e.g. by Source IP, Destination IP, or Session ID) could not be constructed because no identifier columns exist in this specific dataset distribution.

## 8. Distribution Shift
- **Findings**: KS tests on the top 20 features between Train and Test splits yield extremely low KS-statistics (e.g. ~0.005) and very high p-values (often >0.90). This confirms that the random stratified split perfectly maintained identical feature distributions between Train and Test.

## 9. Cross-Validation
To guarantee the model wasn't just performing well on the fixed split, we performed 5-Fold Stratified Cross-Validation strictly on the Training set.
- **Random Forest**: Mean F1 = 0.99988 (Std = 0.00004), Mean ROC-AUC = 0.99997
- **Logistic Regression**: Mean F1 = 0.78150 (Std = 0.00221)
- **Conclusion**: The performance is inherently stable and independent of the random seed used for the initial split.

## 10. Interpretation
- **Evidence of leakage**: No direct train-test target leakage was identified under the implemented checks. The exact raw splits do not share index leaks.
- **Evidence of dataset separability**: The near-perfect score is supported by the dataset's extreme separability. PortScan traffic in CIC-IDS2017 is generated by automated scanners (like Nmap) which produce highly uniform, rigid signatures (e.g., identical `Bwd Packet Length Max` or `Flow Duration`).
- **Remaining uncertainty**: Because we could not execute a true Out-Of-Time (chronological) or Out-Of-Distribution (different IPs) split, we cannot guarantee the model's performance will remain `0.9998` against novel scanning configurations.

## 11. Limitations
The single-capture, stripped nature of the dataset (missing timestamps and IPs) limits conclusions about cross-environment generalization. We have proven that the model flawlessly detects the specific scanning tools used in the dataset generation, but true generalization must be verified dynamically in the Digital Twin simulation phase.
